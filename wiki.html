Survival Guide

Knowledge is your only advantage in the Silent Zone. Learn the mechanics, manage your resources, and stay alive.

1. Core Mechanics

The Day/Night Cycle

Survival is divided into two phases. During the Day, you have 24 Action Points (AP) to spend. You use AP to craft, build shelter upgrades, rest, or travel to scavenge. Once you run out of AP, you must end the day.

The Night

The Night is when the world pushes back. As the day ends, you automatically consume food and water (increasing Hunger and Thirst). Unpatched windows may cause sickness, and the infected or desperate survivors might raid your shelter. Random events also occur, testing your Sanity.

2. Stats & Infection

Core Stats

  • Health: Determines if you survive encounters. Dropping to 0 means death.
  • Sanity: Affected by your actions. High sanity helps you endure, while low sanity leads to madness.
  • Hunger: Drains by 15 every night. If it hits 0, you take Health and Sanity damage from starvation.
  • Thirst: Drains by 20 every night. If it hits 0, you take massive Health and Sanity damage from dehydration.

Infection & Mutations

Infection rises when you take damage from shamblers or consume dirty food/water. As infection crosses certain thresholds, your body mutates:

  • 10% - Heightened Senses: +10% Scavenge bonus.
  • 30% - Unnatural Strength: Stronger in combat, but drains Sanity over time.
  • 50% - Night Vision: Better nocturnal awareness.
  • 70% - Feral Instincts: Faster reflexes, but a heavy toll on the mind.
  • 90% - The Change: A 3-day countdown begins before you mutate completely.
  • 100% - Lost: Game Over. You have turned.

4. Shelter & Crafting

Shelter Actions

  • Clean Debris (4 AP): Scrounge the rubble for scrap, wood, or cloth.
  • Patch Windows (6 AP, 2 Wood): Boarding up windows reduces sickness from the cold and lowers night raid risks.
  • Rest (8 AP): Spend time resting to recover Health and Sanity.

Key Upgrades

  • Improvised Bench: Unlocks crafting for basic tools, bandages, and makeshift weapons.
  • Alcohol Stove: Safely cook food and boil dirty water to prevent infection.
  • Rain Collector & Water Filter: Automates gathering and purifying drinking water.
  • Barricade & Lookout Post: Drastically prevents the odds of your belongings getting stolen during night raids.

5. Scavenging & Combat

Spending AP allows you to travel to different locations within regions like Desolate Town, Industrial Zone, or the Military Checkpoints. Each area varies in Danger Level and loot tables.

Encounters

While scavenging, you will face text-based encounters involving shamblers, trapped survivors, or rival looters. Your choices determine the outcome:

  • Strength Checks: Used to fight or force things open. Boosted by your character stats and equipped weapons.
  • Dexterity Checks: Used to sneak, dodge, or pick locks.
  • Intelligence Checks: Used for dialogue, discovering complex solutions, or careful extraction.

Combat & Weapons

Without a weapon, fighting gives poor odds. Melee weapons (Bats, Machetes, Fire Axes) provide steady combat bonuses. Firearms (Pistols, Shotguns) provide massive life-saving bonuses but require extremely rare ammunition.

6. Items & Gear

  • Food: Range from Canned Food to Rat Meat (which must be cooked). Consuming raw rat meat heavily increases infection risk.
  • Water: Dirty Water must be boiled using an Alcohol Stove; drinking it raw is dangerous. Drink Clean Water to restore thirst safely.
  • Medical: Bandages stop bleeding, Medkits restore huge Health, and Antibiotics/Antivirals cure Infection over time.
  • Scrap & Materials: Essential for building upgrades. Scrap Metal, Wood, Duct Tape, and Nails form the backbone of your survival.
