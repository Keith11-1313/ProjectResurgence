Core Features

Survive Against
All Odds

Every decision matters. Manage your resources, build your shelter, and keep your sanity intact in a world overrun by the infected.

01 / COMBAT
Scavenge & Fight

Explore dangerous locations across multiple regions. Face shamblers, feral dogs, hostile survivors, and worse. Equip melee weapons or firearms — but ammo is scarce.

02 / SHELTER
Shelter Management

Patch windows, build upgrades, clean debris. Your shelter is your lifeline. Reduce raid risk with barricades and lookout posts. Build a workbench to unlock crafting.

03 / CRAFTING
Crafting System

Build an Improvised Bench, then craft weapons and supplies from scavenged materials. Improvised Spears, Nail Clubs, Glass Shivs — crude but lifesaving.

04 / TRADE
Trading & Encounters

Meet wandering traders, desperate survivors, and suspicious scavengers. Trade food for weapons, bandages for guns. But beware — some deals come with an ambush.

05 / WORLD
Four Regions to Explore

Travel between the Desolate Town, Industrial Zone, Rural Outskirts, and Military Checkpoint. Each region has unique locations, loot, and dangers.

06 / INFECTION
Infection & Mutation

Infection spreads through combat and contaminated supplies. As it grows, your body changes — heightened senses, unnatural strength. But at 100%, you turn.

How It Works

A Day in the
Apocalypse

Each day gives you 24 Action Points. Spend them wisely — once they're gone, the day ends and night brings its own horrors.

01

Wake Up & Plan

Check your dashboard — health, hunger, thirst, sanity, and infection. Review your resources and decide: scavenge, craft, or fortify?

02

Scavenge for Supplies

Choose a region and location. Travel costs AP. You'll face a random encounter — shamblers, traps, or friendly traders.

03

Manage Your Shelter

Patch windows to lower sickness risk. Build upgrades for cooking, water collection, and crafting. Rest to recover health.

04

Craft & Equip

Once you build a workbench, combine materials into weapons and bandages. Equip your best weapon — guns hit hardest but burn through ammo.

05

Survive the Night

End the day. Hunger and thirst drain overnight. Raiders may attack. Night events test your sanity. Make it to tomorrow.
